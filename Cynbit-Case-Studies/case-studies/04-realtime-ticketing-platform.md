## Client need

The client needed a ticketing platform that could remain stable during high-demand release events.

## Challenge

Legacy servers frequently crashed during high-demand ticket drops, leading to lost revenue and customer frustration.

## Solution

Cynbit implemented a serverless architecture with elastic scaling and a prioritized queuing system using AWS Lambda and SQS.
