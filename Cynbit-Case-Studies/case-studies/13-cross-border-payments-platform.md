## Client need

The client needed low-latency, compliant cross-border payments at scale.

## Challenge

Existing systems struggled with high latency and complex regulatory compliance across multiple jurisdictions.

## Solution

Cynbit built a microservices-based ledger system using Node.js and PostgreSQL, deployed on an auto-scaling Kubernetes cluster with real-time fraud detection.
