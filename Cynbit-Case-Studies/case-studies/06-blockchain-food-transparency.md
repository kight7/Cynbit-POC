## Client need

The client needed credible, scalable proof of ingredient origin and quality for sustainability-conscious customers.

## Challenge

Customers were increasingly demanding proof of sourcing for organic labels, which was difficult to provide manually.

## Solution

Cynbit built a QR-based tracking system that allows customers to scan a meal and see the entire journey from farm to table.
