## Client need

The client needed to reduce sizing uncertainty and improve purchase confidence in online fashion shopping.

## Challenge

High return rates due to sizing and fit issues were eroding profit margins for the online boutique.

## Solution

Cynbit developed a mobile-optimized AR fitting room that utilizes camera data to provide accurate size recommendations and visual overlays.
