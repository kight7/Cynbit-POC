# AI Diagnostic Triage: Enhancing Medical Efficiency

- **Industry:** Healthcare
- **Name listed by Cynbit:** WizHealth

## Client need

Clinicians needed a reliable way to prioritize a high volume of symptom reports and identify urgent consultations quickly.

## Challenge

Clinicians were overwhelmed with a high volume of symptoms, making it difficult to prioritize urgent cases effectively.

## Solution

Cynbit built an AI model that categorizes symptoms based on severity and urgency, integrating directly with existing patient records.

## Tech stack

**Inferred stack (draft):**

- Python
- Machine-learning classification models
- FastAPI
- PostgreSQL
- EHR/patient-record integration
- React dashboard
- Cloud infrastructure

## Outcome / metric

- Improved triage efficiency by 25% **(Cynbit-reported; unverified independently)**
- Reduced turnaround time for urgent patient consultations **(Cynbit-reported; unverified independently)**
- 99.9% performance **(Cynbit-reported)**
- 24/7 reliability **(Cynbit-reported)**

## AI/ML opportunity

Add retrieval-augmented clinical decision support that surfaces relevant care protocols, red-flag symptoms, and historical case patterns. The system should remain decision support rather than autonomous diagnosis, with clinician review and a complete audit trail.
