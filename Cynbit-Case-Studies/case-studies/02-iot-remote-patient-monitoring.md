## Client need

Rural chronic-care patients needed continuous monitoring and earlier clinical intervention between visits.

## Challenge

Chronic patients in remote areas lacked consistent monitoring, leading to avoidable emergency situations.

## Solution

Cynbit developed a mobile app that syncs vitals from wearable devices to a central clinician dashboard with automated alerts.

## Tech stack

## Outcome / metric

- 40% reduction in emergency-room visits for enrolled patients **(Cynbit-reported; unverified independently)**
- 99.9% performance **(Cynbit-reported)**
- 24/7 reliability **(Cynbit-reported)**

## AI/ML opportunity

Add predictive deterioration detection using longitudinal vitals, adherence signals, and clinical history. The system could identify elevated-risk patients and recommend earlier clinical follow-up while preserving privacy, consent, and clinician oversight.
