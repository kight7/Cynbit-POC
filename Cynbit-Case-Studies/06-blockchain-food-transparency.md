# Blockchain-Lite Transparency for Sustainable Food

**Status:** Draft  
**Industry:** Food supply chain, sustainability  
**Solution type:** Traceability platform, QR-based consumer experience, supply-chain data

## Challenge

Customers increasingly demanded proof of sourcing and quality for organic food products. Manual documentation made it difficult for brands to provide credible, end-to-end supply-chain transparency.

## Solution

Cynbit built a QR-based traceability system that lets customers scan a meal or product and view its journey from farm to table. The platform records key supply-chain events and presents them in a consumer-friendly experience.

### Core capabilities

- QR-code product journey
- Supplier and batch records
- Farm-to-table event timeline
- Verification and provenance data
- Consumer-facing transparency page
- Admin and supplier portal

## Outcome & Impact

- Improved consumer trust through visible sourcing information
- Simplified access to product provenance
- Strengthened brand credibility for sustainable claims
- Created a reusable traceability framework

## Stack

- React / Next.js
- Node.js backend
- PostgreSQL
- QR-code generation
- Cloud storage
- Optional distributed-ledger integration

## AI/ML opportunity

Use AI to validate supplier documents, detect anomalies in supply-chain events, summarize provenance records for consumers, and flag sustainability-claim inconsistencies before publication.
